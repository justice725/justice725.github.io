About ZetaStore

ZetaStore is an independent review blog that looks at health and wellness products sold through the ClickBank marketplace. Every week we pick popular supplements and wellness programs, research the official product pages, and write a plain-English summary of what each product is, what it includes, who it may suit, and what to check before you buy.

What you will find here

  • Product reviews that cover ingredients or components, how the product is intended to work, pros and cons, price range, and the money-back guarantee.
  • Comparisons with similar products in the same category, so you can see alternatives side by side.
  • Buying tips such as how to order from the official site and how refunds work.

How we work

Our reviews are based on publicly available information from the product's official sales page, the ClickBank marketplace listing, and general reference material. We summarize the vendor's claims and label them as claims. We do not test products in a laboratory and we do not make medical judgments.

Who writes these reviews

ZetaStore is run by a small independent editorial team, not by any supplement company or vendor. We are not doctors or dietitians, and every page says so. Each review and guide lists the sources it used in its "How we researched this" box and shows the date it was last checked, so you can judge how current it is. Our ingredient guides summarize published research from sources such as the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements, MedlinePlus and PubMed, and contain no affiliate links.

How we make money

ZetaStore is reader-supported. Links on this site may be affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a commission from the seller at no extra cost to you. This does not influence which products we cover or what we write about them. See our full Affiliate Disclosure.

Important health notice

Nothing on this site is medical advice. Dietary supplements are not evaluated by the FDA to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional before starting any supplement, especially if you are pregnant, nursing, taking medication, or have a medical condition.
