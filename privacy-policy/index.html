Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

ZetaStore ("we", "us") publishes product reviews and comparisons. This page explains what information this website collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We do not require visitors to create an account or submit personal information to read this site. Like most websites, our hosting platform (Google Blogger) automatically collects standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, time on page, and referring website through server logs and cookies.

Cookies and analytics

This site is hosted on Google Blogger, which uses cookies for site functionality, security, and analytics (Blogger stats and, if enabled, Google Analytics). You can control cookies through your browser settings. For details on how Google uses data, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Affiliate links and third-party tracking

Product links on this site lead to third-party sellers and are processed through the ClickBank affiliate network. When you click an affiliate link, ClickBank may set a cookie so that a purchase can be credited to this site. ClickBank and the seller have their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave this site. See our Affiliate Disclosure for more about how we earn commissions.

Email

If you contact us by email, we use your address only to reply to you. We do not sell or share visitor email addresses. If we add an email newsletter in the future, it will be opt-in only and every message will include an unsubscribe link.

Tools used by the site owner

The owner of this site publishes posts using Google's Blogger API through a private application. That application accesses only the owner's own Blogger account and does not collect, store, or share any visitor information.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU or California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Because we do not collect personal data beyond standard server logs held by Google, most requests should be directed to Google. For anything else, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via our Contact page or to cksals123@gmail.com.
