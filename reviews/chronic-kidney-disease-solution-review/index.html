What is Chronic Kidney Disease Solution, and what is included?

The Chronic Kidney Disease Solution is presented by its vendor as a digital lifestyle program created by Shelly Manning. The sales page describes a step-by-step approach organized into three phases: “Protect,” “Restore,” and “Repair and renew.” It discusses lifestyle habits, food choices, gut health, and blood-glucose management. The vendor says the program arrives by email after purchase.

That description is not a detailed product inventory. The page reviewed does not identify a precise number of lessons, downloadable files, video modules, named supplement ingredients, or dosages. It mentions particular foods and natural supplements as part of its narrative, but it does not supply a complete formula or protocol list. Treat the offer as a digital guide or program—not as a bottle of a specifically disclosed, standardized supplement.

The sales page includes personal success stories and broad health claims. Those are the vendor’s marketing statements, not independent verification or typical-results evidence. We have not independently tested the program, reviewed clinical trials of this specific product, or confirmed the stories.

How is the CKD program intended to work?

The vendor says its three-phase plan begins with everyday habit and food changes, then continues with guidance intended to support gut health and stable blood glucose. Its final phase refers to selected foods and natural supplements. In the vendor’s account, these steps are meant to support kidney-related wellness over time. That is a description of the seller’s theory and program—not proof that the program restores kidney function or changes the course of chronic kidney disease.

Important medical distinction: Chronic kidney disease requires individualized medical care. Do not stop or change prescribed medicines, dialysis, fluid limits, or a clinician-directed diet because of sales-page claims. Ask your nephrologist or other qualified healthcare professional to review any proposed food, supplement, or lifestyle changes, especially if you have CKD or take medication.

The page does not provide product-specific clinical-trial results, a full ingredient label, or a medically reviewed treatment protocol. Its own disclaimer says its content is not medical advice and advises readers to consult a healthcare professional. Those limits matter more than promotional language when evaluating a health offer.
