What is BioVanish, and what is included?

WellMe describes BioVanish as a cocoa-flavored drink mix. The vendor says to mix one scoop into 8 ounces of water, milk, or a milk alternative. Its label lists a 6.5-gram serving and 30 servings per container.

BioVanish ingredients per 6.5 g scoop, as printed on the label

Medium-chain triglycerides5 g powder from coconut oil
L-theanine200 mg
Vitamin B6100 mg, as pyridoxine hydrochloride
Folate1,333 mcg DFE, from 784 mcg folic acid
Vitamin B12800 mcg, as cyanocobalamin
Other label details15 calories; 3 g total carbohydrate and 3 g added sugar
Pay particular attention to vitamin B6. The label lists 100 mg in one scoop. NIH's Office of Dietary Supplements gives 100 mg per day as the U.S. adult tolerable upper intake level from all sources. That makes this serving equal to that adult limit before food or other supplements are counted. Ask a qualified healthcare professional or pharmacist whether it is appropriate for you, especially if you take other supplements or medicines.

The sales page calls its main fat component “9-c fats” from coconut extract; the label identifies it as medium-chain triglycerides powder from coconut oil. Taste and mixability preferences will vary.

How BioVanish is intended to work

The vendor's “Dairy Farm Weight Loss Method” centers on beta-hydroxybutyrate (BHB). It claims MCTs support BHB and fat use, while L-theanine supports stress and sleep. These remain vendor claims, not confirmed effects of the finished product.

The sales page refers to university studies and “clinically-backed” amounts but does not provide a BioVanish-specific trial report or protocol. Studies of other ingredients, doses, or diets cannot establish that this product causes weight loss.

What independent sources say about the key ingredients

MCTs and weight management

A 2024 PubMed meta-analysis reported modest average weight reduction in MCT-enriched diets versus long-chain-fat diets. It studied MCT preparations, not BioVanish. NIH's weight-loss supplement review notes ingredient evidence does not necessarily predict a combined product's effect.

L-theanine

A randomized crossover trial of 30 adults studied 200 mg L-theanine daily for four weeks and reported changes in some stress, sleep, and cognitive measures. It did not test BioVanish or weight loss; read the trial at PubMed Central.

B vitamins and weight-loss claims

B vitamins support normal body functions, but their presence does not establish fat loss. See NIH's B12 fact sheet. NIH's B6 fact sheet sets the U.S. adult upper limit at 100 mg daily from all sources. Ingredient research is not proof for this formula.

Red flags and things to verify before you buy

The page uses limited-time and low-stock language, which does not independently confirm inventory or a deadline. It also promotes a $59 one-month price, while visible bundles begin at two jars ($138). Confirm price, shipping, and tax at checkout.

The seller advertises a 180-day “empty jar” guarantee, including refunds after use. The page does not clearly explain claim steps or return postage; it says contact details are on the package. Request written terms and save the confirmation.

The WellMe footer lists (800) 473-2115 and a Bloomington, Minnesota address; the label lists a WellMe distributor address in Tallmadge, Ohio. These may be different offices, but verify the contact details. “Doctor-selected” language does not replace named credentials or product-specific trial evidence.

We did not search FDA warning-letter records or FTC enforcement cases for BioVanish or its seller, so this review makes no claim about whether such records exist.

BioVanish FAQ

What is BioVanish?

It is a cocoa-flavored powdered dietary supplement. The seller says to mix one scoop with 8 ounces of water, milk, or a milk alternative.

What ingredients are on the BioVanish label?

Per 6.5 g scoop, the label lists 5 g MCT powder from coconut oil, 200 mg L-theanine, 100 mg vitamin B6, 1,333 mcg DFE folate from 784 mcg folic acid, and 800 mcg vitamin B12. It also lists 3 g total sugars, all of which are added sugars.

How much does BioVanish cost per day?

Using the displayed bundle prices, 30 servings per jar, and listed U.S. shipping, the amount is about $2.30 per serving for two jars, $1.63 for three, or $1.10 for six, before shipping and tax. Confirm the current checkout total.

Is there a 180-day money-back guarantee?

The seller advertises one and says a full refund may be requested even after the supply is used. The detailed refund steps and shipping terms were not clear on the page reviewed. Confirm the written terms with the seller before ordering.

Is BioVanish proven to cause weight loss?

We did not find an independent clinical trial of the finished BioVanish product in the materials reviewed. Ingredient research cannot establish that this exact blend, dose, and routine will lead to weight loss.

Final verdict: who should consider BioVanish?

BioVanish may suit adults who prefer a cocoa drink mix and value disclosed ingredient amounts. Its 180-day guarantee may help if written terms match the sales-page promise.

Skip it if you require product-specific proof or clear refund terms. Review the 100 mg B6 serving with a healthcare professional, especially if pregnant, nursing, taking medication, or using other supplements.
