What is NeuroPrime and what’s included?

NeuroPrime is sold as a liquid dietary supplement in the health and fitness category. Its sales page positions it as a formula intended to support memory function. That is the vendor’s intended-use description, not proof that the product prevents memory loss or improves a diagnosed condition.

The vendor lists nine plant-derived ingredients: moringa, pine bark extract, ginkgo biloba, tamarind, lion’s mane mushroom, chlorella, bacopa monnieri, spirulina, and neem. The product page calls the formula natural, plant based, non-GMO, and stimulant free. Those are vendor statements; the page does not provide ingredient amounts in the information reviewed, so it is difficult to compare the dose of each ingredient with research or other formulas。

How NeuroPrime is intended to work

The vendor says the combination of nine natural ingredients is designed to support memory function. Its directions say to shake the bottle and take one drop daily, preferably in the morning; it also says the drop may be taken directly or with a beverage. Check the product label you receive, since packaging and directions can change.

The page does not establish that this particular finished formula has been tested in independent clinical trials. A list of ingredients and references about individual ingredients should not be treated as proof that the combined product produces a specific outcome. We found no independently verified performance result to report. View it as a supplement marketed for general memory support, not proven memory-loss prevention or a replacement for professional advice.
