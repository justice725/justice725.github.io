What is VisiFlora and what's included?

VisiFlora is described as a 22‑nutrient vision-support formula that links eye health with gut health. The vendor promotes it as a multi-nutrient capsule designed for daily support, with vegetarian-friendly and non-GMO attributes. The exact amounts per ingredient are not publicly disclosed on the sales page, so review the label at checkout if you need dose specifics.

Key groups named by the vendor include a Vision Defense Matrix (carotenoids and antioxidants), a Gut-Eye Barrier Complex (bioflavonoids and related compounds), and Vision Performance boosters (botanicals) plus a Macular & Lens blend containing lutein/zeaxanthin, beta-carotene, zinc, bilberry, lycopene and saffron.

How is VisiFlora intended to work?

The vendor frames VisiFlora around a gut-eye connection: nutrients absorbed in the gut may support ocular tissues such as the retina and macula. This is the vendor’s proposed mechanism and promotional language; it is not a guaranteed clinical finding. The page references general research but does not provide product-specific clinical trial results for the finished supplement.
