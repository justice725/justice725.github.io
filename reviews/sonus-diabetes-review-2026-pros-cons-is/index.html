What is Sonu's Diabetes Secret?

The official sales presentation describes an educational digital program built around foods, simple recipes, and a three-week meal plan. It frames the material as a way to explore everyday eating habits related to blood sugar.

The sales page says purchasers receive three reports on a computer and by email: Sonu's 9 “Healthy Blood Sugar Level Foods,” Sonu's 5-Minute Recipes for sauces, dressings and dips, and a 21-Day “Always Full” Healthy Blood Sugar Levels eating plan. The plan is presented as covering meals and snacks throughout the day. The page says they can be downloaded or printed.

For people searching “Sonu's Diabetes ingredients,” an important distinction is that this is a food guide, not a supplement with a conventional Supplement Facts panel. The transcript identifies white mulberry. It describes other entries by category—such as yogurt, cabbage, spices, a grain, fruit, and melon—but several are not named in the transcript sections reviewed. Check the materials before buying if you have allergies, dietary restrictions, or medication-related food concerns.

How it is intended to work

The vendor says the approach is to include selected foods regularly and use some of them in quick sauces, dressings, and dips. The vendor describes selected foods and their nutrients as the basis of the plan. These are vendor claims, not independently established outcomes.

Health note: The sales presentation includes dramatic personal stories and health outcome claims. Those are not independent clinical evidence and should not be treated as typical or promised results. Do not change medication, insulin, monitoring, or clinician-directed care because of an online guide.

Is Sonu's Diabetes legit? It is a paid digital guide with stated materials and a checkout offer. That does not validate the more ambitious health claims in its sales copy. We found no independent trial of this specific guide, and we have not tested it. Consider the product for its educational format rather than as a proven diabetes intervention.
