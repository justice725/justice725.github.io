What is Critical T? Ingredients and serving size

Critical T is marketed as a dietary supplement for men's strength, drive and energy. The seller describes its approach as supporting natural testosterone production while helping maintain estrogen balance. Its directions say to take two capsules daily with water. The label says each bottle contains 60 capsules, or 30 two-capsule servings.

The official Supplement Facts label lists these amounts per two-capsule serving:

  • Tongkat Ali root extract — 550 mg
  • Mucuna pruriens seed extract, standardized to 40% L-Dopa — 300 mg
  • Safed Musli root — 200 mg
  • Diindolylmethane (DIM) — 100 mg
  • Eleuthero root extract — 100 mg
  • White button mushroom extract — 100 mg
  • DL-DMAE bitartrate — 50 mg
  • Maca root powder — 50 mg
  • Acacetin — 25 mg
  • L-Tyrosine — 25 mg

The label lists gelatin, magnesium stearate, olive oil and silicon dioxide as other ingredients. It states that daily values are not established for the listed ingredients. One detail worth noticing: the sales page describes “clear, all-natural capsules” and “zero fillers,” while the label lists gelatin and other ingredients. Read the actual packaging and ask the seller about any dietary restrictions.

How Critical T is intended to work

The vendor says Tongkat Ali is intended to support the body's testosterone production. It says DIM may influence estrogen metabolism and describes Acacetin as a way to limit aromatase activity. The seller presents these ingredients as a combined “one-two punch” for hormone balance. Those are seller explanations, not a demonstration that the finished Critical T formula changes hormones or improves symptoms.

The official sales page uses language about energy, libido, motivation and performance. No independent product-specific clinical trial or objective outcome data were provided in the materials reviewed here. Dietary supplements are not a substitute for medical evaluation of fatigue, low libido, or suspected hormone problems.

What independent sources say about the key ingredients

A systematic review and meta-analysis of clinical trials on Eurycoma longifolia (Tongkat Ali) reported an increase in total testosterone in included studies, while noting that further research is needed before clinical use. The studies evaluated Tongkat Ali interventions, not Critical T's blend or its particular formulation. The NIH LiverTox entry also notes that long-term safety is not well documented and discusses rare reports of possible liver injury. Systematic review in Medicina; NIH LiverTox: Tongkat Ali.

An independent review of DIM focuses mainly on mechanisms and research related to breast cancer prevention, including experimental work and human studies. It does not establish that DIM raises testosterone or validates the sales page's claims about this product. Nutrition Reviews: DIM evidence review.

Important distinction: evidence about an ingredient is not evidence about Critical T itself, its exact dose, the combination of ingredients, or the results an individual buyer might experience.
