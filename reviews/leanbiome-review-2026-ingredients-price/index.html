What is LeanBiome and what’s included?

LeanBiome is offered as a dietary supplement in capsule form. The sales page describes a proprietary combination of nine probiotic “lean bacteria” species and Greenselect Phytosome, a caffeine-free green tea extract using Phytosome technology. It also says the capsules use a delayed-release coating intended to protect live organisms through the stomach. These are descriptions from the seller, not independent confirmation that the product produces a particular outcome.

The page names Lactobacillus gasseri, Lactobacillus rhamnosus, and Lactobacillus fermentum among the probiotic species, and says six more are included. The complete remaining strain list and amounts per serving were not clearly available in the product information reviewed. Check the current Supplement Facts label before purchase, especially if you need to avoid a particular ingredient or strain. The sales page also advertises a digital gut-friendly smoothie recipe e-book with some multi-bottle orders; bundle inclusions can change.

How LeanBiome is intended to work

The vendor’s explanation is that gut bacteria and microbiome diversity may be related to appetite, cravings, metabolism, and fat storage. It says combining probiotic strains with green tea extract is intended to support a healthier gut microbiome and weight-management goals. That is the seller’s proposed mechanism. Research on individual ingredients or related strains cannot, by itself, establish that this exact formula causes weight loss, and the sales copy’s outcome examples should not be read as typical or promised results.

In practical terms, think of LeanBiome as a supplement the seller positions as one possible adjunct—not a stand-alone weight-loss plan. Nutrition, activity, sleep, health conditions, and medications all matter. If you are pregnant or nursing, take medication, have a health condition, or are considering a major dietary change, consult a qualified clinician before using a supplement.
