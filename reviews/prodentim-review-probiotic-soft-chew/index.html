What is ProDentim?

ProDentim is described as a dissolvable probiotic soft chew featuring 3.5 billion live probiotic strains, plus a blend of plant-based ingredients. It’s positioned as a convenient way to support a healthy oral environment and fresh breath in addition to daily brushing and dental care.

According to the product page, the formula includes three probiotic strains and a proprietary blend of plant-based ingredients. Real-world results vary, and this product is intended to supplement routine dental hygiene—not replace it.

How to use ProDentim

The manufacturer suggests slowly chewing one tablet each morning to support overall oral health. As with any supplement, do not exceed the stated serving size and consider it as a complement to daily brushing, flossing, and routine dental visits.
