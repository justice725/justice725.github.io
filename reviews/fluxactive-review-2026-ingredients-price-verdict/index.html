What independent sources say about key FLUXACTIVE ingredients

Saw palmetto

The National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health (NCCIH) says a 2023 review of 27 studies found saw palmetto used alone provides little or no benefit for benign prostatic hyperplasia symptoms. The review could not reach definite conclusions about combinations that include saw palmetto. That evidence does not establish whether FLUXACTIVE, or its undisclosed amount of saw palmetto, has an effect. NCCIH: Saw Palmetto.

Ginkgo biloba

NCCIH reports no conclusive evidence that ginkgo is helpful for any health condition, and notes possible interactions, including increased bleeding risk with anticoagulant medicines such as warfarin. This is ingredient-level context, not evidence for the finished FLUXACTIVE formula or its dose. NCCIH: Ginkgo.

Vitamin E

The NIH Office of Dietary Supplements (ODS) describes vitamin E's antioxidant role, but says high-dose supplement forms can increase bleeding risk and may interact with anticoagulant or antiplatelet medicines. Without a readable label and amount, buyers cannot determine how much FLUXACTIVE provides. Evidence about vitamin E is not proof that this product or its dose is effective. NIH ODS: Vitamin E.

FDA explains that supplement manufacturers and distributors are responsible for evaluating product safety and labeling before marketing; FDA can take action against adulterated or misbranded supplements. Read more at FDA: Dietary Supplements.

Red flags and things to verify before you buy

Refund language needs a close read. The main sales page uses broad “100% money back” language. Its detailed policy says unopened items must be returned within 60 days of delivery, all items received must be sent back, original shipping fees are not refunded, and return shipping is paid by the customer. The detailed policy also says opened items may qualify only in limited circumstances, so do not assume the guarantee permits empty-bottle returns.

Confirm what the checkout actually charges. The page calls the purchase a one-time payment with no future payments, and the basic 30-day package lists additional shipping fees. Review the final checkout total and any optional add-ons. We did not verify the live checkout flow.

Promotional urgency is not proof of scarcity. The sales presentation includes time-limited discount and stock-pressure language. Treat countdowns and “limited” availability statements as marketing unless independently verified. The page also uses customer success language and several broad ingredient benefit statements; we found no product-specific trial in the reviewed sales material to substantiate those outcomes.

Company and label details remain incomplete. The product-support email displayed is support@fluxactive.net, and the refund policy gives a return address in Aurora, Colorado. The reviewed sales text did not clearly identify a legal company name, nor did the provided product image show a readable full ingredient label. Verify the manufacturer, complete Supplement Facts panel, lot/quality details, and current return instructions before buying.
