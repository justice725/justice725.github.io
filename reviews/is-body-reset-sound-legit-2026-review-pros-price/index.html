What is Body Reset Sound Formula?

Body Reset Sound Formula is presented as a self-guided digital audio program, not a pill, drink mix or physical device. The vendor describes six “healing songs” pairing soothing music with spoken or sung affirmations, plus a downloadable manual. The page suggests a brief daily listening habit and says the files are MP3s that can be saved to a phone, tablet or computer.

The offer also lists three digital bonuses: an Emergency Craving Crusher, a 3-Minute Mirror Ritual and a Body Reset Journal. It does not give a track-by-track list, exact runtime for every recording or full scripts. The page refers to sessions of roughly five to ten minutes and elsewhere recommends seven minutes a day; these are vendor descriptions.

There is no supplement label because this is audio. The sales page mentions themes such as emotional eating, body shame and old beliefs, but the full song scripts are not published in the material reviewed.

How the method is intended to work

The vendor says the songs pair music with therapeutic words, guided visualization, hypnotic induction and affirmations. It presents listening as a way to reflect on food-related habits and difficult feelings.

The page attributes weight retention to subconscious beliefs and emotional wounds. These are vendor claims, not an established explanation for an individual’s weight. It links no product-specific clinical trial, so do not treat the audio as a substitute for care from a qualified clinician or dietitian.

Important distinction: evidence about music listening in general is not evidence that these particular recordings, their wording or their recommended schedule produce weight loss.

What independent sources say about the method

A 2018 exploratory study in Musicae Scientiae examined survey responses from 571 participants. It found associations between certain music-listening strategies and emotional eating in some subgroups, but associations differed by mood and do not prove that music causes changes in eating. The authors described the results as pointing to a possibility, not a tested weight-loss treatment. Read the study abstract.

A 2021 systematic review and meta-analysis of nine studies reported a small positive relationship between music and food intake. The authors noted the small evidence base, cautioned against generalizing the result and called for more experimental research. This finding does not test affirmations, hypnotherapy-style audio, the Body Reset tracks or long-term weight change. Read the review and meta-analysis.

These sources do not show that music reliably reduces food intake or body weight, and they do not verify the vendor’s “reset” explanation. General findings cannot establish results for this untested product or its specific recordings.

Limitations to weigh
