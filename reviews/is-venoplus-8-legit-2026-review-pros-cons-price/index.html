What is VENOPLUS 8 and what is included?

VENOPLUS 8 is a flavored powder sold under the Simple Promise name. The vendor says it is intended to support healthy blood pressure and cholesterol levels already within the normal range. That is a marketing claim, not evidence that this product changes either measure.

Directions say adults mix one 5.4-gram scoop into 10 fluid ounces of water daily, preferably with a meal or as advised by a healthcare provider. The front image shows a green-apple 162-gram jar. Dividing its weight by the stated scoop size gives 30 servings per jar; this is arithmetic, not a separate serving guarantee.

Ten named ingredients are MenaQ7®, RedNite® beetroot extract, Pomella® pomegranate extract, magnesium, hesperidin, L-citrulline, L-arginine, L-taurine, vitamin C, and grape seed extract. Orders are advertised with a nitric oxide testing guide, thermal therapy guide, and breathwork video.

Label transparency: the supplied product image is only a front-facing jar photo. It does not show a Supplement Facts panel. The pages reviewed list ingredient names but do not provide amounts per serving, full excipient details, or a complete readable label. We therefore cannot compare ingredient doses with research. Ask the seller for the current full label before ordering if those details matter to you.

How VENOPLUS 8 is intended to work

The vendor says the formula supplies nutrients and highlights nitric oxide and blood flow. Beetroot nitrate and amino acids such as L-citrulline are linked to nitric-oxide pathways; that rationale does not establish the finished blend works as advertised.

The vendor recommends one scoop daily with water or a smoothie, preferably with a meal. It describes the product as vegetarian-friendly, lactose-free, gluten-free, nut-free, and without antibiotics. Check the package for allergens because the image lacks a full label.

What independent sources say about key ingredients

Beetroot nitrate and RedNite®: A meta-analysis of 16 short randomized trials found a modest systolic blood-pressure reduction with inorganic nitrate or beetroot juice, but no significant diastolic change. The trials were small and brief; this is not evidence for RedNite® or VENOPLUS 8. PubMed review of nitrate and beetroot trials.

L-citrulline: A 2019 review pooled eight small trials and found a significant systolic, but not overall diastolic, change. No citrulline dose is disclosed, and ingredient evidence is not product evidence. PubMed L-citrulline systematic review.

Magnesium: NIH ODS describes only marginal average blood-pressure reductions in magnesium trials and says cardiovascular effects need more study. The product amount is not disclosed. NIH ODS magnesium fact sheet.

MenaQ7® and vitamin K2: NIH says vitamin K’s role in arterial calcification and coronary risk remains unclear. It can interact seriously with warfarin; consult a clinician before changing intake. This formula’s amount is undisclosed. NIH ODS vitamin K fact sheet. Evidence about any one ingredient does not prove efficacy or safety of this product or its undisclosed dose.

The 365-day guarantee requires mailing bottles back, including used, unused, or empty containers, to the Simple Promise Returns Centre. Include your name and order ID, keep postage proof, and contact support. The policy does not state that return postage is prepaid; processing may take up to two weeks after delivery, followed by 10–14 business days for the bank to post the refund. Read the written return policy before ordering.

The sales page includes testimonials about blood pressure and energy; we have not verified them and do not treat them as typical. It identifies the brand as Simple Promise and gives support number 1-800-259-9522; the related policy lists support@mysimplepromise.com. No formulator credentials are named. Request the full label and confirm postage responsibility.
