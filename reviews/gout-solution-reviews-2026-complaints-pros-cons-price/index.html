What is Gout Solution, and what’s included?

The End of Gout is attributed to natural-health writer Shelly Manning. The vendor describes it as an immediately available PDF/e-book with lifetime access, unlimited downloads, and free updates. A printed-book add-on may be offered after ordering, but its price is not stated.

The listed contents are two quick-start food lists ("eat more" and "eat fewer") and a flexible seven-day plan using supermarket foods. It is presented as food-choice guidance, not calorie or portion tracking. Because it is a digital guide, there is no supplement label, serving size, or servings-per-container information.

Not documented: a complete table of contents, page or recipe count, and research references for each recommendation. We could not independently inspect the ebook itself.

How the program is intended to work

The vendor says readers should choose foods intended to support a healthier gut microbiome and proposes a link between gut microbes, urate processing, and gout. This is the seller’s theory, not verified evidence of this ebook’s clinical effect. Its personal case story is accompanied by a disclaimer that individual results may differ.

NIAMS says lifestyle changes can help manage risk factors, but they are not enough for many people with frequent flares or tophi; clinician-directed urate-lowering medication is central. The ACR guideline also recommends evidence-based medication and flare management. This ebook is not a substitute for medical care.

What independent sources say about the method

NIAMS’s gout treatment guide says limiting alcohol and sugary drinks and following a DASH-style diet may help some people. It also says frequent flares or tophi often require urate-lowering medicine, and advises discussing changes with a doctor.

The 2020 ACR guideline is based on systematic evidence review and recommends clinician-guided urate-lowering treatment for specified groups. It does not validate this particular program.

A 2022 diet-and-gout review discusses diet, metabolism, and gut-related mechanisms. Mechanistic research does not show that this ebook improves gout outcomes. Evidence about a diet or microbiome pathway is not evidence about this product or its protocol; it is not a supplement and specifies no dose.
