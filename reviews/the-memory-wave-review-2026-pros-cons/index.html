The sales copy connects Gamma brainwaves with clarity, focus, and calm thinking, and frames the track as a short routine that may support a sense of mental alertness. Those are the vendor’s explanations and intended-use claims—not independently verified findings about this product.

General discussion of a brainwave pattern does not by itself demonstrate that a particular audio track produces a measurable change or improves memory in everyday life. The page reviewed did not provide product-specific clinical trial results, a published protocol, or a technical description that would let buyers evaluate the sound design. Treat “neuroscientist-created” language as a marketing statement unless supporting details are supplied directly by the seller.
