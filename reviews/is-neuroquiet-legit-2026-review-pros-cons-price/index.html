What is NeuroQuiet? What’s included?

NeuroQuiet is a liquid dietary supplement delivered by oral spray. The vendor markets it for hearing and brain wellness, and associates it with relaxation, mood, and vitality. These are seller claims, not independently verified outcomes. The public sales material does not show a readable supplement label.

The page names Alpha-GPC, GABA, “L-Dopa Bean,” Moomiyo, L-Arginine, and L-Tyrosine. It does not provide amounts per serving, serving size, or servings per container. The supplied image showed bottles, not a readable label, so exact forms and doses could not be confirmed. Although the seller calls the blend “precise,” it does not publish the quantities needed to assess that claim.

Directions say to shake the bottle, use two sprays in the morning and four before bed, hold under the tongue for 20 seconds, then swallow. The seller advises avoiding food or drink for 30 minutes before and after. Ask a clinician or pharmacist about suitability, especially if you take medicine or have a health condition.

Three- and six-bottle bundles include three advertised digital bonuses: Hear Like A Pro, Powerful Ways To Sharpen Your Memory, and Ultimate Tea Remedies. The two-bottle offer has no bonuses. Download length and file formats are not specified. The seller says payment is one-time with no future billing; verify this at checkout.

How NeuroQuiet is intended to work

The vendor links Alpha-GPC to cognition, GABA to relaxation, L-Dopa Bean to dopamine, Moomiyo to vitality, L-Arginine to circulation, and L-Tyrosine to stress resilience. This describes the seller’s rationale; it does not show that the finished spray changes hearing, tinnitus, memory, or mood.

The page includes testimonials and notes some identities were changed. Testimonials and seller-displayed ratings are not controlled studies. Product composition and dose are also relevant, yet incompletely disclosed.

What independent sources say about the key ingredients

Alpha-GPC and choline

Alpha-GPC is a choline-containing compound, but research about choline generally is not direct evidence for Alpha-GPC, the amount in NeuroQuiet, or this product. The NIH Office of Dietary Supplements (ODS) choline fact sheet says findings on choline and cognition are mixed: some studies link higher intake with better cognitive measures, while other studies find no cognitive improvement from supplements. The ODS says more research is needed.

L-Arginine

Mayo Clinic’s L-arginine overview describes evidence in several specific settings, including blood pressure and circulation, and lists possible side effects and medication interactions. That does not demonstrate a hearing or tinnitus benefit. Mayo Clinic advises discussing L-arginine with a clinician if taking blood-pressure medicine, among other precautions.

Evidence about an ingredient is not evidence that NeuroQuiet works, nor does it establish that the product’s dose is effective or safe. The public NeuroQuiet material does not disclose those doses. For this review, we found no product-specific clinical trial report in the sales material reviewed.

NeuroQuiet FAQ

Is NeuroQuiet a capsule or a liquid?

The vendor describes a liquid spray used under the tongue. The public materials reviewed do not identify a capsule format.

What are the NeuroQuiet ingredients?

The sales page names Alpha-GPC, GABA, L-Dopa Bean, Moomiyo, L-Arginine, and L-Tyrosine. It does not provide readable per-ingredient amounts or serving data in the text reviewed.

Can NeuroQuiet stop tinnitus or restore hearing?

The reviewed materials do not establish that it treats tinnitus or hearing loss. The vendor markets the product for hearing support, but that is not proof of a treatment effect. A clinician can help assess persistent or sudden symptoms.

Does NeuroQuiet automatically renew?

The vendor’s FAQ says checkout is a one-time payment with no future payments. Check your live checkout carefully and save the order confirmation.

What does the 90-day guarantee cover?

The seller says bottles, including empty bottles, can be returned within 90 days. Shipping and handling are deducted from the refund. Ask the seller to confirm the current return address and process before returning an order.

Final verdict: who should consider NeuroQuiet?

NeuroQuiet may interest adults who prefer a liquid spray and accept limited label detail. The vendor lists prices, directions, ingredients, bonuses, and a 90-day return promise, but does not resolve questions about exact doses or product-specific effectiveness.

Skip it if you require a full label, independently demonstrated tinnitus results, or a proven hearing treatment. Request the current label and return instructions, and discuss supplement suitability with a healthcare professional.
