What Cardio Shield is and what it includes

Cardio Shield is marketed as an oral dietary supplement featuring botanicals such as hawthorn leaf, olive leaf extract, green tea extract, and garlic, with other ingredients described as part of a synergistic blend. The official sales page does not display a complete Supplement Facts label with serving size or amounts per ingredient. Package sizes are advertised as 30-day, 90-day, and 180-day supplies.

Label availability: The publicly accessible content does not show the full label, so quantities and allergens cannot be verified from the page alone. Verify the current label before purchasing.

How Cardio Shield works (vendor claims)

The vendor describes Cardio Shield as a synergistic blend of botanicals intended to support healthy blood flow and blood-pressure levels. The page emphasizes lifestyle steps such as exercise and a heart-healthy diet, alongside the supplement. No product-specific dose-response or clinical trial is provided on the sales page.

Claims of results typically mention a multi-month horizon; however, these are marketing claims and not a substitute for medical advice or proven outcomes.

What independent sources say about key ingredients

Garlic

Independent sources note that garlic may modestly affect blood pressure in some individuals and highlight possible bleeding risks, especially for people on anticoagulants. See NCCIH's Garlic page for detail: NCCIH Garlic.

Hawthorn

Evidence for hawthorn use is mixed and often limited; safety considerations and drug interactions exist. See NCCIH's Hawthorn page: NCCIH Hawthorn.

Cited ingredient evidence does not validate the finished Cardio Shield product or its specific dosage. Independent sources provide context but not proof of finished-formula efficacy.
