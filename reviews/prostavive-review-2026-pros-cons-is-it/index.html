What is ProstaVive and what’s included?

ProstaVive is presented by its vendor as a dietary supplement for men, sold as a powder rather than a conventional pill. The sales page recommends mixing one scoop with water or a preferred beverage once daily. It also says to take it with a meal. Follow the current package directions if they differ; this page is not a dosing recommendation.

The vendor names eleven ingredients: boron, Tongkat Ali, ashwagandha, fenugreek, Panax ginseng, maca root, artichoke extract, nettle root, zinc, magnesium, and vitamin D. The first eight are described as botanical or mineral ingredients, while the final three are common nutrients. The sales page also provides access to a Supplement Facts image, but amounts were not legible in the text available for this review. Check the actual label for serving sizes, quantities, allergens, and other ingredients before purchase.

Orders of three or six bottles are advertised with two digital bonuses: a prostate-health smoothie guide and a healthy-sex-drive plan. These are vendor-described extras, not a substitute for individualized clinical guidance. The offer may change, so confirm bundle contents in the current checkout.

How ProstaVive is intended to work

The vendor says its formula is intended to support prostate wellness and healthy blood flow, and describes nitric-oxide-related circulation as part of its rationale. It also associates the ingredients with urinary wellness, energy, sleep, and male vitality. Those are promotional claims. They should not be read as proof that this supplement improves symptoms, prevents disease, or produces a predictable result.

Evidence check: The sales page references research but does not establish that the finished ProstaVive blend itself has been tested in a controlled clinical trial. We found no independent product trial in the materials reviewed. Individual ingredient research does not prove the finished product will deliver the same effects.

Seek medical care for persistent urinary symptoms, blood in urine, pain, or difficulty urinating. Ask a clinician or pharmacist before using this multi-ingredient formula, especially if you take medication or have a health condition.
