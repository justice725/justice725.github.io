What is Neura (Super Legit Memory Supplement)?

Neura is presented by its vendor as a brain-health dietary supplement in capsule form. Its marketing describes support for memory recall, focus, mental clarity, alertness, and cognitive energy. These are vendor-described wellness aims, not established outcomes for every buyer. This page summarizes the available sales-page information and is not a clinical assessment.

The product page identifies Lion’s Mane mushroom (Hericium erinaceus), wolf or goji berry, Bacopa monnieri, and shilajit extracts. The reviewed text does not state ingredient amounts or provide a full Supplement Facts panel. Check the package label for serving size, other ingredients, allergen details, and quantities.

Neura ingredients and how the vendor says it works

The vendor says the formula supports neurotransmitter balance and cholinergic function, antioxidant protection, and membrane integrity and signaling between neurons. These are proposed mechanisms, not proof that this formula produces a measurable cognitive effect or prevents decline.

The vendor links Bacopa with memory and learning, Lion’s Mane with focus and brain wellness, goji berry with antioxidant support, and shilajit with energy and stamina. No product-specific clinical trial or independently verified dose data are provided. Consult a clinician before starting any supplement, especially if you have a health condition or take medication.
