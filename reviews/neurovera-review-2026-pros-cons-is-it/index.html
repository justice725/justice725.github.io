What is NeuroVera, and what’s included?

NeuroVera is a dietary supplement sold online in bottle bundles. The vendor positions it as a natural formula intended to support memory function and overall brain health. The product page says a bottle contains 60 capsules and describes a one-month supply. It recommends two capsules per day with 6–8 ounces of water; check the current bottle label for complete directions before use.

The sales page says the blend contains nine natural ingredients, but the ingredient section names only five: Schisandra fruit extract, Gotu Kola powder, Shilajit extract, Lion’s Mane mushroom, and Bacopa monnieri. It does not identify the remaining four in the list reviewed or give the amount of each ingredient per serving. That missing detail makes it harder to compare the formula with research or assess whether a dose matches a studied ingredient.

The seller calls it plant-based, non-GMO, and stimulant-free; these are vendor statements, not independently verified findings. "Natural" alone does not establish safety or effectiveness.

We have not independently tested NeuroVera or verified manufacturing claims. Prices and terms can change.

How NeuroVera is intended to work

The vendor says its natural ingredients are intended to support memory and overall brain health, but it does not explain a specific mechanism or cite a clinical trial of the finished formula. Research on individual ingredients would not, by itself, prove that this blend improves memory. Treat "memory support" as an intended-use claim, not a demonstrated result.
