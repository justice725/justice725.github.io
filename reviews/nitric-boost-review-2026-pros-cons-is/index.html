What Is NITRIC BOOST and What's Included?

NITRIC BOOST is sold as a powdered dietary supplement, not a prescription medicine. The vendor lists eight ingredients: beet root powder, L-arginine, L-citrulline DL-malate, niacin (vitamin B3), horny goat weed, ginkgo biloba powder, dong quai, and D-aspartic acid. The page describes a proprietary blend but does not clearly provide quantities for all eight ingredients. Check the Supplement Facts label and checkout disclosures before deciding.

The vendor says to take one scoop with water, tea, or a smoothie each day. It describes the product as vegan, dairy-free, non-GMO, and made in the United States, and states that manufacturing is at a GMP-certified facility. Those are vendor-provided product details, not independently audited findings from this review. The page advertises two digital bonuses with three- and six-bottle orders. The offer, bonus availability, and shipping terms can change.

How NITRIC BOOST Is Intended to Work

The vendor says the ingredients are intended to support the body's nitric-oxide pathway and healthy blood flow. It describes beet root as a source of dietary nitrates, L-arginine and L-citrulline DL-malate as amino-acid support, and several botanicals as supporting circulation or male vitality. These are the seller's explanations, not proof that this product improves erections, testosterone, or sexual function.

The page reviewed does not present a product-specific randomized clinical trial or plainly state the dose of each active ingredient. Research on an individual ingredient, when available, is not the same as proof for this exact blend. Erectile or sexual-function changes can have medical causes. A healthcare professional can help assess symptoms and possible medication or supplement interactions.
