What is Spartamax? Ingredients and what's included

The vendor presents Spartamax as a daily male vitality gummy for adults interested in libido, stamina and sexual performance. These are seller claims, not proof of a medical effect. The sales page calls the formula closely guarded.

The vendor lists L-Arginine HCl, Tongkat Ali, Maca Root, Ashwagandha, Horny Goat Weed, Beet Root and Grape Seed Extract. The reviewed page has no readable Supplement Facts panel, quantities, serving size or servings per bottle. Its ingredient artwork is not a full label, so dose comparisons and cost per serving cannot be calculated.

One bottle is described as a 30-day supply. Three- and six-bottle orders include two digital bonuses: Spartan Surge Blueprint, a guide with tips, foods and smoothie recipes, and Dominant Sexual Power, a program about sexual confidence and techniques. The page gives no length, format or author credentials for these bonuses.

How Spartamax is intended to work

The vendor says its blend is designed to support arousal, drive, firmness and overall performance. It combines seven botanicals and an amino acid in a proprietary gummy, but publishes no quantities or Spartamax-specific clinical study on the page reviewed. Broad claims about the finished supplement are not established by research on an individual ingredient.

Spartamax is not a substitute for care for erectile dysfunction, low libido or other symptoms. Discuss persistent or sudden changes with a qualified clinician.

What independent sources say about the key ingredients

L-Arginine

NIH ODS describes evidence for arginine as an exercise-performance enhancer as limited and conflicting, with studies generally finding little or no effect. This is not evidence about sexual function or Spartamax; its amount is not disclosed for dose comparison. NIH ODS: Dietary Supplements for Exercise and Athletic Performance.

Ashwagandha

NCCIH notes that studies used varied ashwagandha preparations and flags possible side effects, rare liver-injury reports and medication interactions. It advises against use during pregnancy and for people with thyroid or autoimmune disorders or hormone-sensitive prostate cancer. This does not establish Spartamax's preparation or dose. NCCIH: Ashwagandha—Usefulness and Safety.

Ingredient research is not product proof. Evidence about an ingredient does not show that Spartamax works, and it cannot establish the safety or effectiveness of this particular blend at undisclosed doses. Ask a healthcare professional about possible interactions before using a supplement, especially if you take medication or have a health condition.
