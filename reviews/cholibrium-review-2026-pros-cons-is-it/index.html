What is Cholibrium and what’s included?

Cholibrium is a capsule supplement marketed for cardiovascular and antioxidant support, including healthy heart function and cholesterol levels already within a healthy range. The marketplace listing targets adults 40+; its sales page says men and women interested in cardiovascular wellness.

The formula is presented as a blend of 10 mushroom extracts: reishi, lion’s mane, cordyceps, shiitake, maitake, turkey tail, chaga, royal agaricus, white button mushroom, and black fungus. Three listed bonuses cover dessert recipes, smoothies and juices, and reading food labels. The reviewed page did not show extract amounts or a full Supplement Facts panel; verify the label if those details matter to you.

The vendor says each bottle contains 60 capsules, or a 30-day supply. Its directions are to take two capsules daily, preferably 20–30 minutes before a meal with an eight-ounce glass of water, or as advised by a healthcare provider. The page describes the product as non-GMO, gluten-free, allergen-free, and manufactured in a cGMP-certified facility. Those are vendor statements, not independent quality testing by this reviewer.

Cholibrium ingredients: how it is intended to work

The vendor says the mushroom extracts are intended to complement the body’s natural cardiovascular processes and provide antioxidant support. It highlights reishi and shiitake for maintaining cholesterol levels in a healthy range, cordyceps for cardiovascular support, and turkey tail and other mushrooms for antioxidant-related wellness. These descriptions explain the seller’s intended positioning; they do not establish that the finished Cholibrium formula produces a particular health outcome.

The sales page references traditional use and ingredient research, but this review did not verify a Cholibrium-specific clinical trial. Ingredient research elsewhere is not proof for this finished formula or its doses. The vendor says the claims have not been evaluated by the FDA and the supplement is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease.
