What is iGenics?

iGenics is a dietary supplement promoted for vision and eye-health support. The vendor positions it as a plant-based formula for people who want nutritional support for their eyes, particularly as they get older. This is a supplement review, not a medical recommendation, and the product should not be confused with an eye exam, corrective lenses, or treatment for an eye condition.

The vendor says the formula contains 12 ingredients, including nutrients associated with an AREDS-2 formula, but the accessible product page does not enumerate all 12 or provide a complete supplement facts panel. Ask for the full label if you need to check allergens, doses, or interactions.

iGenics ingredients: what the vendor names

The sales page names Ginkgo biloba, Bilberry, Saffron, and Turmeric with BioPerine. It also refers to \u201cAREDS-2\u201d ingredients and describes added vitamins and nutrients, but the page text reviewed here does not supply a complete, verified 12-item list with amounts. Do not infer the missing ingredients or assume the formula matches a research formulation just because the page uses the AREDS-2 name.

The vendor says iGenics is made in a U.S. GMP-certified facility and that ingredients are third-party tested. These are seller statements, not independently verified here; ask for quality documentation if it matters to your decision.

How iGenics is intended to work

The vendor says iGenics is designed to support clearer vision, a healthy inflammatory response, and nutrient intake; it also attributes antioxidant effects to Ginkgo biloba. These are marketing claims, not proof that iGenics improves eyesight or treats an eye disorder.

Ask a qualified clinician before use if you have a condition, take medication, or are pregnant or nursing. Do not delay an eye exam or stop prescribed care because of supplement advertising.
