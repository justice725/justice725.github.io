What is TitanFlow, and what's included?

TitanFlow is a dietary supplement marketed to men for urinary-flow and prostate support. Its sales page emphasizes the urethral lining; that is the seller's positioning, not independently established evidence about the finished formula.

The five named ingredients are pumpkin seed oil, beta-sitosterol, lycopene, broccoli sprout extract, and pygeum. The vendor describes pumpkin seed oil and beta-sitosterol as a paired source of urinary support; it presents lycopene and broccoli sprout extract as antioxidant ingredients and pygeum as a traditional herbal extract. The overview does not disclose the amount of each ingredient, so shoppers should inspect the current Supplement Facts label before purchase and compare it with their clinician's advice.

TitanFlow comes in bottles, not as a drink mix. The seller says to take two capsules daily with the first meal; one bottle is a 30-day supply. Three- and six-bottle orders include two digital guides: “Sleep Well for Urinary Health” and “Quick & Easy Meals for Prostate Support.” Confirm bonus terms at checkout.

How is TitanFlow intended to work?

The vendor says its proprietary blend is intended to support urinary flow and complete bladder emptying, with particular emphasis on the strength of urethral walls. It attributes potential support to the ingredients above and refers on its sales page to research about pumpkin seed oil and urinary measures. This review has not independently verified the cited research, the formulation doses, or whether research on an individual ingredient applies to the finished TitanFlow product.

Marketing explanations are not proof of disease treatment. Outcomes are not guaranteed. Seek medical advice for persistent, painful, or worsening urinary symptoms.
